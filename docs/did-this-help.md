# Did This Help?

Community feedback helps distinguish **useful mitigations** from unsupported causal claims.

## Tell us the outcome

After trying a safe step, reply in the closest existing Discussion with one of these outcomes:

- **HELPED — recovered after normal waiting**
- **HELPED — another already-authorized client worked**
- **PARTIAL — behaviour changed but the problem remains**
- **NO CHANGE**
- **RECOVERED WITHOUT INTERVENTION**
- **UNKNOWN — I did not test further**

If useful, add only: the symptom, broad platform family, whether the failure was intermittent, and whether normal waiting changed the result.

## What feedback means

“Helped me” is a mitigation observation. It does **not** prove why the failure happened. A repeated pattern can justify further investigation, but frequency in this repository is not prevalence across ChatGPT.

## What not to do

Do not repeat a failing action merely to test it again. Do not automate retries or reconnects. Do not publish private identifiers or logs just to support feedback.
