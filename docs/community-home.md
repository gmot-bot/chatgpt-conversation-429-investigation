# Community Help Centre

**Having trouble with ChatGPT conversations or history? Start here. No technical knowledge required.**

> Unofficial community project. We cannot access your ChatGPT account and we are not OpenAI Support.

## Choose one thing

### 1. I need help now
Go to [Community Help](../HELP.md) and choose the message or symptom you see.

### 2. I want to know whether other people see this
Open [Other people are seeing this](people-are-seeing-this.md). Similar symptoms are grouped without assuming a shared cause.

### 3. I want a safe workaround
Open [Workarounds & Solutions](workarounds-and-solutions.md). A workaround that helps is not automatically a root-cause fix.

### 4. I want to report what happened
Use the **Community help question** or **Simple problem report**. Technical logs are optional.

### 5. I have technical evidence
Use the **Independent reproduction** form and the [technical evidence framework](evidence-quality-hypothesis-matrix.md).

Do not post passwords, tokens, cookies, email addresses, account/conversation/request IDs, IP addresses, precise location, private chats, private support correspondence or unsanitized HAR files.
