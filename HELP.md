## Four safe choices

1. **Find your error** in the [Error & Problem Library](docs/error-library.md).
2. **Try only low-risk steps** in [Safe troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
3. **Ask the community** in [Discussions](https://github.com/gmot-bot/chatgpt-conversation-429-investigation/discussions/1).
4. **Report what happened** with the GitHub **Simple problem report** if your observation may help others.

## Before you post

You do not need to publish logs to ask for help. Never post passwords, authentication tokens, cookies, email addresses, account/conversation/request IDs, IP addresses, precise location, private chat content, private support correspondence or unsanitized HAR files.

Do not repeatedly refresh, automate retries, create reconnect storms, generate load or bypass rate limits to obtain evidence.

## What this community can and cannot do

We can compare symptoms, point to similar public reports, document safe workarounds, separate confirmed observations from hypotheses, and improve the public evidence base. We cannot inspect your account, promise recovery, confirm a universal root cause, or replace OpenAI Support.

**Current investigation status:** root cause **NOT DETERMINED**. Similar-looking failures may have different causes.
